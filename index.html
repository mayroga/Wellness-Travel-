Audio, privacy and limitations

Browser speech recognition may use a browser or operating-system service. It can capture your voice and background sounds too; it cannot reliably guarantee that only the other speaker is transcribed. Noise reduction and echo cancellation are not perfect voice isolation.

Recognized text is sent to the configured AI provider when a reply or translation is requested. This app does not upload audio recordings to its own FastAPI server or save transcripts on the server.

Use transcription in accordance with applicable consent and privacy laws. Verify suggested replies before making important commitments.
